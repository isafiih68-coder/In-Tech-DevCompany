# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The In.Tech community aims to provide a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Give feedback about ideas and work, not personal characteristics.
- Respect privacy.
- Help keep discussions relevant and collaborative.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Abusive or persistently hostile communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through an official project contact channel.

Reports will be reviewed carefully and handled confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action in response to conduct that violates this Code of Conduct.
